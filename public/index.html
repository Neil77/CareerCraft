Why CareerCraft?

The system is useful because it reduces the initial confusion involved in career exploration. A student can enter their interests and skills and immediately see relevant career options, the skills associated with those careers, and a possible learning direction. It acts as a starting-point career exploration tool rather than replacing human career counselling.

How it works

  1. Explore or chooseDiscover matches or pick a career you already want.
  2. AssessAnswer 24 short scenarios across eight transferable skills.
  3. AnalyzeSee your score in each skill, weighted for your career.
  4. LearnFollow a path built from your biggest career-relevant gaps.
  5. TestTake a short quiz on that skill.
  6. TrackWatch your progress on the dashboard.
