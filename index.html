Why CareerPath?

Most students know what they study but not how strong their everyday skills are. CareerPath makes that visible and gives you a next step.

🔍

Discover

Answer short, situation-based questions and see where you are strongest.

📊

Analyze

See your score for each skill and the areas with the most room to grow.

🚀

Improve

Follow a step-by-step learning path and check your progress with quizzes.

How it works

  1. AssessAnswer 12 questions about how you work.
  2. AnalyzeSee your score in each skill.
  3. LearnFollow a path built from your lowest scores.
  4. TestTake a short quiz on that skill.
  5. TrackWatch your progress on the dashboard.
